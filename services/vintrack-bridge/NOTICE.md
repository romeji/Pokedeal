# Vintrack attribution

This bridge is an original, reduced integration inspired by the architecture,
catalog response models and bounded polling design of
`JakobAIOdev/Vintrack-Vinted-Monitor` (MIT License, copyright 2026
JakobAIOdev). It deliberately does not vendor Vintrack's dashboard, proxy
manager, TLS fingerprint client, account service or notification stack.

Upstream: https://github.com/JakobAIOdev/Vintrack-Vinted-Monitor
